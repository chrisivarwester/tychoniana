The original

IOANNES GEORGIUS HERWARTUS AB HOHENBURG AD TYCHONEM BRAHE.

ACCIPIES, Vir nobilissime atque Mathematicorum omnium longe præstantissime, Catalogos ambos librorum, qui in his nundinis vernalibus Francofurti ad Mœnum venales extiterunt. Si quos ex his petas, velim eos mihi designari, priusquam exemplaria divendantur, et curabo sedulo, ut quamprimum eos accipias. Mitto etiam tabulas ELISABETHÆ Reginæ Angliæ, GEMMAM FRISIUM de radio Astronomico, et Astrologiam græcolatinam IOACHIMI CAMERARIJ No. 106. Tabulas perpetuas longitudinum et latitudinum Planetarum Lovanienses, et Theoricas Planetarum PURBACHIJ ab ORONTIO FINÆO emendatas No. 99. Atque hisce me tibi commendo, teque florere quam fœlicissime exopto.

Datæ Monachij tertia die Aprilis Anno 1600.

English translation machine translation, Secure

Johann Georg Herwart von Hohenburg to Tycho Brahe.

You will receive, most noble man and by far the most outstanding of all mathematicians, both catalogues of the books which were on sale at this spring fair at Frankfurt am Main. If you should seek any from these, I would wish them designated to me before the copies are sold off, and I will diligently see to it that you receive them as soon as possible. I also send: the Tables of Elizabeth, Queen of England; Gemma Frisius On the Astronomical Radius [the cross-staff]; and the Greek-Latin Astrology of Joachim Camerarius, No. 106; the Louvain Perpetual Tables of the longitudes and latitudes of the Planets; and the Theoricae Planetarum of Peurbach, emended by Orontius Finaeus, No. 99. And with these I commend myself to you, and most heartily wish you to flourish most happily.

Given at Munich, the third day of April, in the year 1600.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Johann Georg Herwart von Hohenburg (see brahe-t_16000923001), Chancellor of Bavaria, forwarding both Frankfurt spring-fair (nundinae vernales) book-catalogues and a consignment of works from Tycho's desiderata list.
  • Books. Tabulae Elisabethae Reginae Angliae — astronomical tables dedicated to / associated with Queen Elizabeth I of England (identification flagged: most plausibly a set of ephemerides or tables bearing the dedication; not securely pinned to one edition). Gemma Frisius de radio Astronomico = Gemma Frisius, De radio astronomico et geometrico (1545) — on the cross-staff (radius astronomicus, per glossary). Astrologia graecolatina Ioachimi Camerarij = the Greek-and-Latin astrological compilation of Joachim Camerarius the Elder (No. 106). Tabulae perpetuae … Lovanienses = the Louvain Tabulae perpetuae of planetary longitudes and latitudes. Theoricae Planetarum Purbachij … ab Orontio Finaeo emendatae = Georg Peurbach's Theoricae novae planetarum, in the recension corrected by Oronce Fine (Orontius Finaeus) (No. 99).
  • The "No." numbers (106, 99) are entries on Tycho's standing book-want list (see the Munich series).
  • Calendar. Munich (Catholic Bavaria) = New Style; tertia die Aprilis 1600. Header agrees with the label.
