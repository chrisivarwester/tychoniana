The original

NOBILISSIMO DOCTISSIMOQUE VIRO, DOMINO TYCHONI BRAHE, DOMINO A KNUDSTRUP ET URANIBURGO, MATHEMATICORUM OMNIUM FACILE PRINCIPI, DOMINO ET AMICO SUO SINGULARI OBSERVANTIA COLENDO.
PRAGÆ.
IN ABWESEN HERRN ALEXANDER PRENDTL FÜRSTL. BAYER. AGENTEN ABZUEGEBEN.

MITTO, Vir Nobilissime atque Mathematicorum omnium facile princeps, his iunctos libros ex ijs, quos desideras, APOLLONIJ Pergæi Opera No. 73 et Tabulas BLANCHINI, PRUGNERI et PURBACHIJ No. 34. Tibique fœlicissima quæque ex animo precor et opto.

Datæ Monachij die 23 Sept. A. 1600.
D. T. Nobilmæ
Addictissimus
IOAN. GEORG. HERWART AB HOHENBURGK.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the most noble and most learned man, Master Tycho Brahe, Lord of Knudstrup and Uraniborg, easily the prince of all mathematicians, his lord and friend to be honoured with singular respect.
At Prague.
In the absence of Herr Alexander Prendtl, the Bavarian ducal agent, to be delivered.

I send, most noble man and easily prince of all mathematicians, joined herewith, these books out of those that you desire: the Works of Apollonius of Perga, No. 73, and the Tables of Blanchinus, Prugner, and Peurbach, No. 34. And I pray and wish you from my heart every happiness.

Given at Munich, the 23rd day of September, in the year 1600.
Most devoted to Your most noble [self],
Johann Georg Herwart von Hohenburg.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Johann Georg Herwart von Hohenburg (1553–1622), Chancellor of Bavaria at Munich, jurist and learned polymath who corresponded with both Tycho and Kepler; here acting as Tycho's book-procurer at the Frankfurt fairs and the Munich/Augsburg market.
  • Delivery. The German address-line directs that the packet be left, in his absence, with Alexander Prandtl (Prendtl), the Bavarian ducal agent (fürstlich-bayerischer Agent) at Prague — the standard conduit for Herwart's consignments to Tycho. (The Prague Bavarian agency is the link confirmed in the Tycho-thesaurus Prague layer.)
  • The "No." numbers (No. 73, No. 34) are items on Tycho's own book-desiderata list, which Herwart was working through; they recur across this Munich series (see brahe-t_16000403001, …0418001, …0426002, …1127001).
  • Books. Apollonij Pergaei Opera = the Works (chiefly the Conics) of Apollonius of Perga. Tabulae Blanchini, Prugneri et Purbachij = the astronomical tables of Giovanni Bianchini (Blanchinus), Nicolaus Prugner, and Georg Peurbach (Purbachius) — the same trio Tycho asks after again in November (brahe-t_16001127001).
  • Calendar. Munich (Catholic Bavaria) = New Style; die 23 Sept. 1600. Header agrees with the label.
