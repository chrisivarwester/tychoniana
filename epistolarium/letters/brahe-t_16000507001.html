The original

MAGNIFICO ET NOBILISSIMO VIRO, DOMINO IOHANNI BARVITIO, SACRÆ CÆSAREÆ MAIESTATIS SECRETARIO SUPREMO ET A CONSILIJS INTIMIS, DOMINO ET AMICO HONORANDISSIMO.

MAGNIFICE ET NOBILISSIME VIR, DOMINE ET AMICE HONORANDISSIME.

QUONIAM ea, de quibus Maga Va mihi per domesticum meum, quem ultimo Pilsnæ habui, spem fecit, brevi effectum exoptatum habitura, adhuc infecta hæreant, et frustra hac præterlapsa septimana cum Sacra Cæsa Maiestate, Domino nostro Clementissimo, Væ Magæ accessum a multis pariter speratum per aliquot dies Pragæ expectarem, cogor denuo (siquidem res ulteriorem moram vix patiatur, et incertus sim, quam cito Sa Cæsa Matas eo redire decreverit) eundem familiarem ad vos amandare, ut si per alias multiplices occupationes fieri nunc commodius quam antea possit, plenariam aliquam resolutionem obtinere queam, quod Magæ Væ favorabili intercessione me impetraturum confido. Quænam vero illa sint, de quibus adhuc sollicitus detineor, is, quem amando, Væ Magæ rectius et plenius, quando ipsum audire commodum fuerit, exponet, ut non opus sit multis verborum ambagibus, eam alias occupatissimam hisce literis ulterius interpellare. Rogo autem Magam Vam officiose, quemadmodum semper sua benevolentia mihi præsto fuit, sic etiam nunc non deesse velit. Inveniet me semper pro mea tenuitate eius vicissim studiosissimum et observantissimum.

Valeat Maga Va quam diutissime et quam felicissime.
Dabantur ex arce Benaticka die 7 Maij Anni 1600.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the magnificent and most noble man, Master Johann Barvitius, Supreme Secretary of His Sacred Imperial Majesty and of the privy councils, lord and most honoured friend.

Magnificent and most noble man, lord and most honoured friend.

Since those things — about which Your Magnificence gave me hope, through my servant, whom I last had with [me] at Pilsen, that they would shortly have the wished-for effect — still stick undone, and since this past week I waited in vain, for some days at Prague, for Your Magnificence's access to His Sacred Imperial Majesty, our most clement Lord (an access equally hoped-for by many): I am compelled again — since indeed the matter scarcely suffers further delay, and I am uncertain how soon His Sacred Imperial Majesty has resolved to return thither — to send the same servant to you, so that, if it can now be done more conveniently than before amid your manifold other occupations, I may obtain some full resolution; which I trust I shall obtain through Your Magnificence's favourable intercession. But what those things are, about which I am still held anxious, he whom I send will set forth to Your Magnificence more rightly and fully, when it shall be convenient to hear him — so that there is no need, by many windings of words, to interrupt you (otherwise most occupied) further with these letters. But I ask Your Magnificence dutifully that, as your benevolence has always been at hand for me, so now too you would not be wanting. You will find me always, for my slenderness, in turn most zealous and most observant of you.

May Your Magnificence fare as long and as happily as possible.
Given from the castle of Benátky, the 7th day of May, in the year 1600.

Apparatus

  • Recipient. Johann Barvitius (Barwitz), here styled Secretarius Supremus and a consiliis intimis of His Imperial Majesty — Rudolf II's privy secretary and the gate-keeper of access to the Emperor (the same Barvitius to whom Elector Ernst had written on Tycho's behalf, brahe-t_15980626002).
  • Content. Tycho, still awaiting a "full resolution" (plenaria resolutio) of his affairs (his stipend and standing) more than a year into his Bohemian residence, presses Barvitius to expedite matters: he had waited in vain at Prague for Barvitius's audience with the Emperor, and now sends his servant again, since "the matter scarcely suffers further delay." The servant — not the letter — will explain the particulars. A characteristic glimpse of the slow, frustrating Rudolfine bureaucracy.
  • Pilsnae = Pilsen (Plzeň), where Rudolf II's court had withdrawn (recurrently, on account of plague at Prague); Tycho's servant had last met Barvitius there.
  • Abbreviations. Maga Va = Magnificentia Vestra ("Your Magnificence"); Sacra Caesa Maiestas = Sacra Caesarea Maiestas; a consiliis intimis = "of the privy councils [council secretary]."
  • Calendar. ex arce Benaticka die 7 Maij 1600 = from the castle of Benátky, 7 May 1600; Bohemia (imperial) = New Style. Header agrees with the label.
