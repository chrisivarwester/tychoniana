9Sources

Every source the register cites, with its full reference, what it contributes to this chapter, the records that cite it, and an open-access or catalogue link where one was verified. A source with no link is one for which no open copy could be found; the gap is stated, not hidden. The evidence grades of §1 are the register’s, not the sources’: a grade B record may rest on an excellent study the compilation has simply not read at the page.

    Contributes The 2010 exhumation as a project — who, when, what was sampled, and when the results were announced.
