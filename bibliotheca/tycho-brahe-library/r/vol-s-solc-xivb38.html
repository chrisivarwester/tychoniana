Na titulní straně přípisek jezuitské klementinské koleje v Praze datovaný 1636.
Na titulní straně přípisek klementinského matematického muzea. (The catalogue records the Jesuit college of 1636/1638 and the Mathematical Museum as former owners and no Tycho note; the 'T O' initials and the verse annotations are Šolc's reading of the volume.)
Chain of custody
  1. Tycho’s heirs, Prague 1601–1636
    The library remained with the heirs when the instruments and the observation books were ceded to Rudolf II and taken to Vienna in 1604; during the long proceedings over the unpaid purchase price they were forced to part with their possessions piecemeal, and parts of the library passed into other hands: one part as a gift to the Jesuit college in the Clementinum, whose volumes carry the accession note of 1642, the Chomutov college’s Tychoniana apparently in the same way, and single works into private ownership (Kleinschnitzová 1933, pp. 75–76 and 78, after Volf 1931). — Kleinschnitzová 1933; Norlind 1970
  2. Jesuit college in the Clementinum (Collegium Caesareum), Prague — the Mathematical Museum 1636–1773
    The college’s inscription dated 1636 on the title and the Mathematical Museum’s inscription (the library’s record, which gives the college of 1636/1638 and the Museum as former owners; Šolc 2002). The volume carries no ‘Ex Bibliotheca Tychoniana’; it reached the college six years before the group of 1642. — NKP, Staré tisky (Aleph); Šolc 2002
  3. The Clementinum library after the suppression of the Society: the public University Library, since 1990 the National Library of the Czech Republic, Prague from 1773
    The college library passed to the state with the suppression of 1773 and became the public University Library in the Clementinum; the Tychoniana were mixed with the other mathematical books and reassembled by the librarians around 1900 (Šolc 2002, p. 329). — Šolc 2002
