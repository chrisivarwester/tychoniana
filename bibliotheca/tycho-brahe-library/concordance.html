The concordance aligns each entry with the numbers under which the earlier catalogues list it: Prandtl’s Verzeichnis A (the works) and Verzeichnis B (the bound volumes, Bände I–XXIX), Kleinschnitzová’s FK numbers and the shelfmarks she recorded, Šolc’s numbers, the page of Norlind’s Bilaga I, Zinner’s number where given, the VD16 number, the National Library’s record number, and the letter in Tycho’s correspondence that attests the book. Sort by any column to read the concordance from any catalogue’s side. Prandtl’s A 6 (an Archimedes of Basel 1548, his Band IX) answers to no edition and is read here as a misdating of the Hervagius Archimedes of 1544 in Band VIII; it has no entry.

Below the table the concordance is read from the earlier catalogues’ side: every number of Prandtl’s Verzeichnis A and B, every volume and item of Kleinschnitzová, Šolc’s additions, every entry of Norlind’s Bilaga I and every record of the National Library’s former-owner index under Brahe, each answered by the entry or volume it stands for here, or by the reason it has none; and the register of the entries and records this edition has retired, with the reason and the record that absorbed them.
