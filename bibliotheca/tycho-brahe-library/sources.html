  3. Bargum 1906–07
    G. Bargum, ‘Über Tycho Brahes Buchdruck und Bucheinbände’, Zeitschrift für Bücherfreunde 10 (1906/07), Beiblatt (Chronik), pp. 124–126, signed ‘Kopenhagen. G. Bargum’. A reply to the binding note of Heft 1 and to Kukula: the Uraniborg press was at work by 1584, not 1596; the plaques served as the mark of Tycho's library and as his printer's and publisher's device; Hasselberg's three binding types; the German library copies of the Mechanica; the Zwinger volume ‘kein schweizer, sondern ebenfalls ein dänischer, unter Tychos persönlicher Anleitung hergestellter’ binding, though the fourteen medallions have no parallel on his other bindings — Tycho was not at Basel in 1587, and Gellius Sascerides, who set out in the summer of 1588, may have procured the book at Basel or at the Frankfurt fair; Kukula's conjecture of late luxury bindings answered with the Ramus and Maestlin–Clavius volumes; and the suggestion that Tycho took from Zwinger's title the name of his projected Theatrum astronomicum.
    Contributes The first argument that the Röhsska binding is Danish work, and the Theatrum astronomicum remark.
    Available online Internet Archive

  13. Císařová Smítková, Paličková & Vackářová 2021
